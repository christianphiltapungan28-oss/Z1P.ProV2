WHY Z1P.Pro

Built for people who want to think it through.

Most SI just hands you an answer. Z1P.Pro helps you understand why, so the decision, and the growth, stay yours. Every chat you have with Zion & Zita becomes part of a bigger picture of where you are and where you're heading.

HOW IT COMES TOGETHER

Three places where your growth happens.

Everything in Z1P.Pro connects: what you talk about becomes what you do, and what you do shows up in your profile.

01

Conversations

Talk through what's on your mind at any hour. Z1P helps you slow down, look at the situation from a new angle, and find your own next step.

02

Turn any conversation into a journey: something you want to accomplish or do with your life. Break it into steps and come back to it whenever you're ready.

    GOOD TO KNOW

    A few questions.

    The things people ask before they start talking with Zion and Zita.

    Who are Zion and Zita?

    They're the two Z1P.Pro companions. Pick the one you'd like to talk with. Either one helps you think a situation through and turn it into a journey with clear next steps.

    Is there an iPhone app?

    Not yet. Z1P.Pro is available for Android phones now. Follow Z1P.Pro on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn or TikTok to hear about new releases.

    Is the Free plan really free?

    Yes. Free costs nothing, forever. It includes 20 Super Intelligence chats a day, 1 active journey and basic life metrics.

    Do paid plans renew automatically?

    No. When a paid period ends, your account goes back to Free. You decide if and when to pay again.

    Who can get a Student plan?

    Anyone with a school email ending in .edu.ph. Pay monthly, or once per 5-month semester and save.

    How do I install the Android app?

    Download the app file and open it. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser (Settings → Install unknown apps), then tap Install.
