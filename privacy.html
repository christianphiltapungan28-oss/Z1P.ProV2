Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Z1P.Pro is operated by FlowSmart. This page explains what we collect when you join the Z1P.Pro waitlist and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, which you type into the waitlist form.
  • The time you signed up.
  • Your IP address, which is checked briefly to limit repeated sign-ups and block spam. We do not store it with your signup.

How we use it

  • To send you a confirmation email when you join.
  • To email you about early access and our launch.
  • To make sure each email address joins the waitlist only once.

We do not sell your email address or use it for advertising.

Who handles your data

We use trusted services to run the waitlist: Supabase stores the list, Vercel hosts this website, and an email provider delivers our messages. They process your data only to provide those services.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your email address at any time, or stop receiving our emails, by writing to FlowSmart@flowssmart.com. We will remove it from the waitlist.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Keeping it safe

Your data is sent over an encrypted connection, and the waitlist database accepts new sign-ups only. The public cannot read it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
